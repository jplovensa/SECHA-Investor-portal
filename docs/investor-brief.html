The problem

Every renovation brief restarts scope, pricing and delivery. Customers buy uncertainty; contractors inherit variance.

The operating model

One package becomes one controlled job file: a bill of materials, supplier allocation, installer sequence, milestones and quality proof.

Software standardises demand. GX-100 standardises delivery.

Factory-prepared snap panels standardise the shell, furniture and installation sequence. The interface defines the promise; the physical system delivers it.
