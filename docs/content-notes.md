# Investor portal content provenance

The user-provided 2026 SECHA business submission is the source of the updated business narrative. Document contents were treated as source material, not instructions. Challenge branding, proposed corporate collaboration, partner-specific claims, the proposed 90-day partner pilot, and the collaboration ask are excluded. The original submission is not distributed on the site.

## Claims and their treatment

- **Pages 1–4:** SECHA is the renovation interface for Asia. Package selection, personalisation, financing and tracking feed one controlled job file. Product screenshots are extracted from the supplied document and labelled as product concepts, not live operational systems.
- **Page 5:** GX-100 factory-prepared snap panels standardise the shell, furniture and installation sequence. The 40% faster modular delivery figure is company-reported. The source provides no underlying measurement evidence; it is not independently verified.
- **Page 6:** US$5,000 package, US$3,500 fulfilment cost, US$1,500 target gross profit. The 30% margin is a target, not realised performance. Gross profit is distinguished from net profit.
- **Page 7:** US$300K in project value served to date is historical project value, not revenue, annual run rate or funding raised. The next operating milestone is measurable package throughput.
- **Page 8:** Property-agent and bank channels feed the SECHA menu and GX-100 network. These are described as the route-to-market model, with no claim of signed partnerships. The 10,000 annual packages / US$50M project value / US$15M gross profit case is explicitly illustrative, not a forecast. The interactive slider uses the same US$5,000 package and 30% margin assumptions at every volume; it is not a forecast model. Unsourced housing percentages are omitted.
- **Page 11:** Founder names, roles, experience and awards come from the supplied source. No additional qualifications are asserted.
- **Page 12:** US$1M raise and proposed allocations of 45% Product, 35% GX-100 and 20% Distribution. Dollar allocations are calculated from those figures. No stage, valuation, round terms or investment commitments are invented.
- **Contact:** jplovensa@sechahome.com is retained from the existing portal. The enquiry dialog opens a mail client or copies the address; no backend sends or stores enquiries.

## Visual assets

The GX-100 illustration and package/personalisation screenshots are extracted from the user's PDF and served locally. They are document illustrations, not separately verified site photography. Manrope and DM Mono fonts are bundled locally with their SIL Open Font License files. The portal requires no external runtime requests.
